## Reconciled current facts

- Current Super 5G starter kits still begin at **€1**. Physical starter kits are available through the Telia e-store; eSIM starters are available through the Super app.
- Both physical Super SIM and eSIM starter kits can only be **activated in Estonia**. After activation, the line can travel abroad with roaming enabled.
- Calling-card services remain valid for **180 days** from activation or from the last **single top-up of at least €3**. Splitting €3 into smaller top-ups does not extend validity.
- After the 180-day period there is a **30-day rescue window** in which incoming calls/messages can still be received/read and the account can be topped up. Without one qualifying ≥€3 top-up, the number and remaining prepaid funds are lost.
- Online/app top-up supports card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and internet bank; retail and Telia-contract-number top-up channels also remain available.
- Roaming must be enabled before travel. EU service follows the Estonia price list subject to fair-use rules. Outside the EU, operator prices vary; this review does not claim universal free incoming SMS or guaranteed OTP reachability.
- Super's current basic standard-rate call/SMS price changed to **€0.05** effective 24 September 2025. This does not change the number-retention rule: the documented reset event remains a single ≥€3 top-up.

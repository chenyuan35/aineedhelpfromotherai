## Evidence-quality scale

- **Policy:** public provider-stated constraint; valid for provider terms, not proof of field outcome.
- **First-hand / one account:** a dated author's own purchase, device or SMS experience; supports *that event*, not a pass rate.
- **Independent contradictory accounts:** different reporters with different outcomes; no inferred population frequency.
- **Unknown:** required link in the requested end-to-end user job is untested, unspecified, or only asserted by third parties.

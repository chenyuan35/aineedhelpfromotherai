Status: **HOLD / RETENTION RESOLVED, ONBOARDING NOT YET ADMISSION-READY**

## Scope

Resolve the remaining haha SIM admission blocker after the keep-alive rule was settled: current activation, real-name registration, initial app onboarding and operational reliability.

This review does not publish the route and does not change the existing UK Phone production surface.

## Current first-party state

Current Fortress material establishes a real supported workflow:

- The current Fortress haha SIM product is sold as a reusable physical SIM with HKD 50 stored value and is listed in stock on Fortress's Hong Kong store.
- Initial SIM activation is performed by inserting the SIM into a handset and receiving the activation SMS / assigned mobile number; Fortress explicitly says to perform initial activation with Wi-Fi off.
- First haha TRAVEL App login requires the SIM ICCID printed on the card/barcode.
- Most account operations, including balance, packages, top-up history, number retrieval and real-name registration management, are handled in the haha TRAVEL App.
- Fortress says Hong Kong local service requires real-name registration and a local package.
- OFCA continues to list A.S. Watson Retail (HK) Limited / Fortress haha SIM as a regulated prepaid-SIM registration provider and points users to the Fortress haha SIM route and haha TRAVEL App.
- Current Fortress real-name registration accepts Hong Kong ID or a valid travel document for users without a Hong Kong ID; registration data includes name, date of birth, document number and document copy.
- Fortress's current product page describes real-name registration as compulsory for Hong Kong service.

## Important registration / reliability boundary

Do not publish haha SIM as a dependable "no-KYC" route.

Current official material is nuanced rather than a clean no-KYC promise:

- the product page says real-name registration is compulsory for Hong Kong service;
- current Fortress support material recognizes SIMs that have not completed real-name registration, which means unregistered account states can exist;
- however, current Fortress support also warns that real-name-registration spot checks can suspend service, including SMS reception, until verification/review is completed;
- the current real-name-registration terms state prepaid-SIM registration requirements and allow Fortress to suspend local service when required information is not completed or cannot be validated.

For Phone Radar purposes, the safe operating model is therefore: **treat real-name registration as a required reliability step for a route intended for long-term number ownership and OTP/SMS use, even if some overseas-use states may technically function before registration.** Do not market the route around avoiding registration.

## Admission decision

**Remain HOLD / Watch.**

The route is commercially real and the official workflow is now sufficiently mapped, but the remaining confidence gap is operational rather than documentary.

The missing admission evidence is one current, independently attributable end-to-end reproduction covering most of this chain:

`new physical card -> activation SMS / number assignment -> haha TRAVEL login -> real-name registration with a valid supported document -> stable mainland/overseas network registration -> receive at least one ordinary SMS/OTP -> verify balance/expiry in app`

A current failure/recovery reproduction would also be useful if it shows that a new-card ICCID mismatch or registration failure can be resolved predictably through Fortress support.

Until that exists:

- do not publish haha SIM as admission-ready;
- do not claim guaranteed no-KYC overseas use;
- do not claim dependable OTP compatibility from seller copy or old tutorials;
- keep the resolved `HKD 10+ -> +365 days` retention rule as valid research state;
- keep the current HKD 50 Fortress acquisition route and app-based management workflow as first-party facts;
- show the 2026 app/onboarding failures as active confidence-lowering evidence if/when the route is later admitted.

## Next trigger

Re-open admission only when the watcher or manual research finds a fresh independent 2026+ end-to-end activation/registration outcome, preferably from a user activating a new card outside Hong Kong or immediately before leaving Hong Kong. No production change is warranted before that trigger.

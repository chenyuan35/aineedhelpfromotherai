## Current commercial and retention facts

O2's latest tariff **effective 2026-10-05** confirms a **CZK99 GO na den physical starter with CZK100 starting credit**, and **CZK29/day** charged after the first outbound call or mobile-data connection; separately billed SMS does not itself activate the daily package, nor does passive inbound SMS. The **prepaid SIM/number is valid 12 months since the last top-up**. Standard **CZK300** is the current minimum online/store top-up. This is a **cash-to-credit commitment** instead of line-rental expense. Standard **CZK300–499 top-up credit remains spendable for only six months**; **CZK500+ remains valid for 12 months**. Number lifetime is distinct from unused-credit expiry; remaining credit lapses without compensation on expiry. Avoid accidental billable data/voice use if keeping the GO na den line dormant.

## SIM/eSIM, registration and activation

The low-entry starter is physical SIM. Current O2 support for **prepaid SIM-to-eSIM replacement explicitly requires visiting an O2 store**, with SIM/PUK and identity (passport/ID); standard replacement **CZK99**. Generic O2 online eSIM signup flows are not proof of direct new GO prepaid eSIM acquisition from China. A 2024 Czech regulator paper acknowledges anonymous prepaid SIMs as a general category, but does not establish unconditional KYC-free onboarding for this exact O2 product. Foreign applicant identity requirements, offshore first activation and reliable remote eSIM conversion are unverified. An independent 2024-08-17 traveler described purchasing an O2 prepaid SIM in a Prague store; exact SKU/OTP are not proven.

## Funding and overseas use

O2 top-up support confirms **CZK300–9,999 online** by compatible Visa/MasterCard/Maestro, with an explicit requirement that the card be internet enabled and **issued in Europe**. Shops allow cash or card. Do not assert Chinese/other non-European cards work. Oct 2026 terms explicitly state **free inbound roaming SMS/MMS** and ordinary GO prepaid supports roaming with partners, subject to partner access and plan entitlements; selected Datamanie data-only products differ. O2 Wi-Fi Calling does **not** handle SMS/MMS; it is not an offshore OTP workaround. A China roaming destination entry is provider capability, not an independent proof of a particular bank/app verification delivery. No exact-route service observations are created.

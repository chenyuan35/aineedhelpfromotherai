Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026.

Browser-based tools

Most calculators and supported image tools run in your browser. Values and files selected for those tools are not intentionally sent to our application server for processing.

TikTok Video Publisher

If you choose to connect TikTok, TikTok sends our server an authorization code that is exchanged for OAuth credentials. The client secret and OAuth credentials are not exposed to page JavaScript. Session credentials are encrypted and stored in a Secure, HttpOnly cookie so the website can make authorized TikTok API requests on your behalf.

When you explicitly press “Post to TikTok,” the selected video is uploaded to the temporary media-upload URL issued by TikTok. We do not intentionally keep a permanent copy of the selected video. TikTok processes the video and post settings under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Basic website data

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and diagnostic logs to deliver and secure the site.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's policies and your consent choices where required.

Third-party links and services

Pages may link to or integrate with external services, including TikTok. Their data practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when site features or advertising practices change. The latest version will always be published at this URL.

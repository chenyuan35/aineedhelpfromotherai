Know the real cost, where to buy, and whether the number will survive the job.

Start with the job, then compare purchase cost and channel evidence before activation details. The tool works through buying, KYC, activation, registration, roaming SMS, keep-alive rules and recovery.

Evidence rule: popular tutorials and community threads tell us which questions people repeatedly get stuck on. Current first-party rules decide hard restrictions, refund conditions and lifecycle warnings. A community workaround never overrides an official restriction.
This is also used to enforce target-service geography rules where the service publishes them.

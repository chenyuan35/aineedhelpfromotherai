Quick answers

Long-term number or temporary SMS?

Use a long-term route when future recovery matters. Temporary SMS is for short-lived jobs where losing the number later is acceptable.

Does a data eSIM give me a phone number?

Often no. Data-only products are shown separately so you do not mistake internet access for an SMS-capable mobile number.

Why is there no fake “97% OTP success” score?

A precise percentage needs enough current, route-specific outcomes. Until then Phone Radar uses qualitative signals instead of pretending sparse reports are statistics.

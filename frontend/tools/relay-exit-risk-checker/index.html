How the Exit Risk Index works

The index is not a shutdown probability. It is a transparent weighted summary of observable signals. Measured availability can contribute up to 50 weight points, well-supported pricing signals up to 20, and the 90-day community forecast up to 30 once at least three votes exist. Yes maps to 100, Unsure to 50 and No to 0 for the community survival score; the actual Yes/Unsure/No shares are shown separately. If a current, non-stale third-party snapshot explicitly marks a site dead, the index is floored at 95. Source snapshots older than 36 hours are down-weighted instead of treated as fresh.

Missing evidence lowers confidence instead of being filled with a fake neutral score. The index is deliberately not presented as a calibrated probability: calibration requires a history of forecasts paired with resolved outcomes. Model-trust votes are shown separately and do not currently change the exit-risk index.

Why prepaid exposure matters

A relay can work today and still be a poor place to park a large balance. The calculator converts your balance into days of normal usage. A seven-day balance is easier to replace than a sixty-day balance if the service changes, pauses, or disappears.

Privacy and evidence limits

This page never asks for your relay API key. We store a normalized relay hostname, a random browser voter ID, the 90-day forecast, optional model-trust sentiment, and timestamps. Third-party measurements retain source attribution and capture time. Crowd opinion is never presented as proof of fraud, insolvency, or model substitution.

Frequently asked questions

Does a high index prove a relay will disappear?

No. It summarizes observable risk signals and community forecasts. It is not a factual accusation or a calibrated probability of shutdown.

Where does the measurement data come from?

The first automatically ingested source is Sinan Compute's published open-data feed. We save only normalized summary fields and daily snapshots with attribution. Other specialist sites are linked for independent cross-checks.

Does model-trust sentiment affect the exit-risk score?

Not currently. It is displayed separately. A suspicion vote is not evidence of model substitution; use specialist verification tools for reproducible checks.

Can I change my forecast?

Yes. Voting again from the same browser for the same relay updates that browser's previous forecast.

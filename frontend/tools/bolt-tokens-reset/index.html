Paid token allowances reset on the subscription renewal date, have no daily token cap, and subscription tokens can roll over for one additional month. Source: Bolt Help Center ↗

Free vs. paid Bolt token resets

Bolt documents different reset schedules by plan. Free users receive a fixed monthly allowance that resets on the first of each calendar month. Paid subscribers receive their next token allocation on the subscription renewal date, so the paid reset date is account-specific.

The Free plan has two limits

Bolt currently lists 1 million tokens per month and a 300,000-token daily usage cap for Free. Hitting the daily cap can stop work even when the monthly balance still shows tokens remaining. Bolt does not document one universal daily reset clock, so the live account remains the source of truth.

Paid rollover changes the planning math

Paid subscription tokens can roll over for one additional month and remain valid for up to two months, while Free tokens do not roll over. Bolt consumes older rollover buckets first. This calculator focuses on the next allocation reset; check the Bolt subscription page for the age of individual rollover buckets.
